Auction grades — what they mean

Every auction car carries an inspection grade. We check it before bidding, so you know exactly what you're buying.

Overall grade

  • 5 / 4.5 Excellent — like new
  • 4 Good condition, light wear
  • 3.5 / 3 Average — visible wear or repairs
  • 2 / 1 Poor — needs work
  • R / RA Accident-repaired history

Interior grade

  • A Excellent, clean
  • B Good, minor marks
  • C Noticeable wear or stains
  • D / E Heavy wear or damage

💡 Most happy buyers choose grade 4 or above, interior B or better. We'll advise you honestly — even if it means waiting for a better car.

FAQ

How does on-demand sourcing work?

You send the make, model, year range and budget through the request form. We search local stock and the Japanese auctions, find matching cars, and reply with photos, condition details and one total price. You only pay when you accept a car.

Can you buy a car for me at auction?

Yes. Tell us the car and your maximum budget. We bid on your behalf, translate the inspection sheet before bidding, and arrange delivery. Auction fees and transport are included in the price we quote — no surprises afterwards.

How do I sell my car to TFI Japan?

Send the “Sell my car” form with your car's details. We value it against the local market and recent auction results, then offer a direct purchase price — or, if you prefer, we can sell it at auction for you.

Do you deliver outside your area?

Yes — delivery within Japan can be arranged for any car we sell, and the cost is included in your quote before you decide.

Is it free to make a request?

Yes. Requests and valuations are free, with no obligation to buy or sell.
