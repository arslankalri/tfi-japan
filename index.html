FAQ

How does on-demand sourcing work?

You send us the make, model, year range and budget through the request form. We search local stock and the Japanese auctions, find matching cars, and reply with photos, condition details and one total price. You only pay when you accept a car.

Can you buy a car for me at auction?

Yes. Tell us the car and your maximum budget. We bid at the auction on your behalf, translate the inspection sheet, and arrange delivery. Auction fees and transport are included in the price we quote you — no surprises afterwards.

How do I sell my car to TFI Japan?

Send the “Sell my car” form with your car's details. We will value it against the local market and recent auction results, then offer you a direct purchase price — or, if you prefer, we can sell it at auction for you.

Do you deliver outside your area?

Yes — delivery within Japan can be arranged for any car we sell. The cost is included in your quote before you decide.

Is it free to make a request?

Yes. Requests and valuations are free and there is no obligation to buy or sell.
